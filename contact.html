FAQ

Questions about working with an interior design agency.

What type of projects does DA Design Visuals take on?

+

We specialise in residential homes, content-first studios, offices and boutique hospitality spaces. If your project needs thoughtful layout, light planning and strong visual storytelling, it’s likely a good fit.

Do you only work in Pune?

+

Our core team is based in Pune, but we collaborate on projects across major Indian cities with trusted on-ground partners. For outstation projects we combine virtual workshops with scheduled site visits.

What is the typical timeline for an interior project?

+

For a 2–3 BHK home, design can take 4–8 weeks and execution 10–16 weeks depending on scope, approvals and site readiness. Studios and offices vary more, but we share a clear roadmap before we begin.

Can you work with my existing contractor or vendor team?

+

Yes. We can either execute with our ecosystem of partners or collaborate with your preferred contractors, carpenters and vendors—as long as quality benchmarks and timelines are aligned.

What budget range should I have in mind before reaching out?

+

Every project is different, but we’re the right fit when you’re ready to invest in long-term design rather than quick fixes. Share an honest range in the form and we’ll tell you what’s realistically possible.
